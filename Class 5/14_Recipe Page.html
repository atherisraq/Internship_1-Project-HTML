Biriyani Recipe

Published: 30 September, 2026

Ingredients

  • Rice - 2 cups
  • Chicken - 500g
  • Onions - 2 large
  • Tomatoes - 2 medium
  • Yogurt - 1 cup
  • Spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala)
  • Ghee or oil - 3 tbsp
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves for garnish

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat ghee or oil in a large pot and fry the onions until golden brown.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until browned.
  4. Add tomatoes, yogurt, and spices. Cook for 10 minutes.
  5. Add the soaked rice and enough water to cook the rice. Cover and cook on low heat until done.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander and mint leaves before serving.
